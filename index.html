One long-horizon bioinformatics task, a model's answer, and how I'd grade it

Ulcerative colitis transcriptomics on public GEO data, through to a LINCS L1000 drug-repositioning query.

This task is built on the pipeline from my MSc thesis in Bioinformatics and Biostatistics: four GEO cohorts, 228 samples, per-cohort limma, a cross-platform RankProd meta-analysis and a LINCS L1000 query with signatureSearch. Every decision point below is one I had to get right myself.

I also turned the thesis's main limitation, the lack of an independent validation cohort, into the held-out test of the task.

Task prompt

Using only the three Affymetrix cohorts below, build a consensus gene signature of active ulcerative colitis versus healthy mucosa. Freeze it. Then (a) test whether it reproduces in the Illumina cohort GSE114527, which you must not open until the signature is frozen, and (b) query LINCS L1000 for compounds that reverse it. Report every sample-selection rule, the integration method and why you chose it, the held-out result, and the top compounds with how you validated the ranking.

Deliverables

  • Sample sheet with an inclusion or exclusion reason for every GSM.
  • Per-cohort differential-expression tables and the frozen UP/DOWN signature, with thresholds stated.
  • Held-out report for GSE114527.
  • LINCS ranking with a positive-control check, and one paragraph on limitations.

Decision points the answer has to get through

  1. Who counts as "active UC"? Trap: GSE38713 labels remission and non-involved mucosa as UC too; GSE47908 contains dysplasia. A practitioner keeps involved, active samples only and logs each exclusion.
  2. Probe annotation on the PM array. Trap: GSE87466 IDs look like 1007_PM_s_at, so hgu133plus2.db matches almost nothing unless the infix is stripped. The failure is silent; the check is the annotation rate per cohort.
  3. Several probes per gene. State the rule (maximum |log2FC|, mean, or most variable) and its bias: maximum |log2FC| keeps real signal from weak probes but can inflate fold changes when probes disagree.
  4. Integrating platforms. Trap: jointly normalising Affymetrix and Illumina intensities. A practitioner runs limma per cohort (eBayes, Benjamini–Hochberg) and combines ranks across cohorts, e.g. RankProd on the intersected genes.
  5. Freezing before testing. The signature (genes, directions, thresholds) is fixed before GSE114527 is opened. Its Illumina file must have the Detection P-value columns split off before log2 and quantile normalisation, and the duplicate column (suffix _a) removed.
  6. Building the query. 100–200 genes per direction ranked by significance, converted to Entrez IDs; expect some to drop out because L1000 measures 978 landmarks and infers the rest.
  7. Reading the score. Trap: sign. Reversal means a negative connectivity score. The positive control is approved small molecules for UC; biologics are barely in L1000, and hits profiled in HT29 (colon) deserve more weight than MCF7 or A549.
Backtracking point. If the frozen signature reproduces poorly in GSE114527, the expected move is to re-check sample selection and annotation (decision points 1–2) before touching thresholds. Tuning thresholds on the held-out cohort is leakage.

Model answer

Illustrative and condensed: written to show the failure modes I see most often in this kind of task, not copied from a specific model. Click a marker to jump to its review note.

Response
I downloaded the series matrices for all four cohorts and merged them into a single expression matrix1. After quantile normalisation across all samples I removed platform effects with ComBat and fitted limma for UC versus control, with eBayes moderation, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR < 0.05 and |log2FC| ≥ 12. For GSE38713 I used every sample annotated as ulcerative colitis3. Probes from all Affymetrix cohorts were annotated with hgu133plus2.db4.

The signature is large and robust: GSE114527 confirms it, since most signature genes are also significant there5. I then queried LINCS L1000 with the top 150 UP and 150 DOWN genes; the best repositioning candidates are the compounds with the highest positive connectivity scores6. Infliximab and vedolizumab were not recovered, so the positive control failed and the ranking should be treated with caution7.

Annotated errors

Critical 1 · The held-out cohort is used to build the signature

What
GSE114527 was merged in before anything was frozen.
Why it matters
Every later "validation" on that cohort is circular. This alone invalidates the claim in marker 5.
Fix
Build on the three Affymetrix cohorts only, freeze the signature, then open GSE114527 once.

Major 2 · Right test, wrong data model

Credit
limma with eBayes, BH correction and explicit thresholds is the correct engine for microarray intensities.
What
Quantile-normalising Affymetrix and Illumina intensities together and then running ComBat.
Why it matters
Intensities from different platforms are not on a common scale, and the UC/control balance differs by cohort, so batch correction can remove real biology or create it.
Fix
Run limma per cohort, intersect annotated genes, and combine ranks across cohorts (e.g. RankProd), which makes no shared-distribution assumption.

Major 3 · Remission and non-involved mucosa counted as active disease

What
"Every sample annotated as ulcerative colitis" in GSE38713 includes remission and non-involved biopsies.
Why it matters
It dilutes exactly the active-inflammation signal the task asks for.
Fix
Keep involved, active samples (15 UC vs 13 controls) and log every exclusion by GSM. The same applies to the dysplasia samples in GSE47908.

Major 4 · Silent annotation loss on the PM array

What
GSE87466 IDs carry a _PM_ infix that hgu133plus2.db does not recognise.
Why it matters
Most probes in the largest cohort fail to map without an error, which shrinks the shared gene universe for every later step.
Check
ids  <- featureNames(eset)             # "1007_PM_s_at", ...
fix  <- sub("_PM_", "_", ids)           # "1007_s_at"
mean(!is.na(mapIds(hgu133plus2.db, fix, "SYMBOL", "PROBEID")))
Report this rate before and after the fix, for every cohort.

Critical 5 · Circular validation, measured the wrong way

What
"Most signature genes are also significant in GSE114527."
Why it matters
Circular because of marker 1. Even without leakage, per-gene significance in a cohort with 6 controls is underpowered and says little about the signature as a whole.
Fix
Report direction concordance of the frozen signature and of the named markers, and how well a signature score separates UC from controls in the held-out cohort.

Critical 6 · Connectivity read with the wrong sign

What
Candidates ranked by the highest positive score.
Why it matters
A positive score means the compound mimics the disease signature. The list returned is the opposite of what was asked.
Fix
res <- result(gess_lincs(qsig, sortby = "NCS", tau = TRUE))
head(res[order(res$NCS), c("pert", "cell", "NCS")])  # most negative = reversal

Major 7 · Wrong positive control

What
Missing infliximab and vedolizumab treated as a failed control.
Why it matters
L1000 profiles are mostly small molecules in cancer cell lines; monoclonal antibodies are essentially not represented, so their absence says nothing about the pipeline.
Fix
Check that approved small molecules (corticosteroids, tofacitinib) score NCS < 0, and weight hits profiled in HT29 (colon) above MCF7 or A549.

Feedback to the model

Your differential-expression settings are sound, but the result can't be trusted yet. First, keep GSE114527 out until the signature is frozen; your validation currently tests the data you trained on. Second, read connectivity scores with the correct sign: reversal is negative NCS, so your candidate list is inverted. Before rerunning, restrict GSE38713 to involved, active samples, strip the _PM_ infix before annotating GSE87466 and report annotation rates, and integrate the cohorts with per-cohort limma plus a rank-based meta-analysis instead of joint normalisation. Use approved small molecules as the positive control.
